## Content type: candidate-interview

This CMS instance runs the candidate-interview type — see the "Type:
candidate-interview" section of `CONTENT.md` for its topics, templates, and
rules. Type skills in `.claude/skills/`: `interview-setup` (bootstrap an
engagement), `interview-round` (add and debrief a round),
`interview-rehearsal` (mock round), `interview-refresher` (gap course +
exam). `exercises/` holds runnable coding drills outside the CMS layers.
