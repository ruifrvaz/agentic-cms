## Content type: recruiter-interview

This CMS instance runs the recruiter-interview type — see the "Type:
recruiter-interview" section of `CONTENT.md` for its topics, templates, and
rules. Type skills in `.claude/skills/`: `recruiter-setup` (bootstrap a
requisition), `recruiter-round` (design a round or take-home; calibrate
interviewers), `recruiter-candidate` (intake, score, reference-check,
archive a candidate), `recruiter-decision` (compare candidates, record
decisions, make offers). Candidates are referred to by opaque slug
everywhere outside their own C2 pages; candidate documents are never stored
in `raw/`. `exercises/` holds take-home assignments outside the CMS layers.
